### 王智超 Zhichao Wang

研究员、博士生导师。现任建科环能科技有限公司专业总工、建筑环境与能源检测院副院长，兼任中国建筑科学研究院空气调节研究所副所长；全国暖通空调及净化设备标准化技术委员会（SAC/TC143）委员。

长期从事暖通空调与热泵设备的检测评价和标准化工作，研究空气源热泵现场性能、部分负荷能效与数据驱动预测；参与起草 GB/T 14296《空气冷却器与空气加热器》、GB/T 14294《组合式空调机组》等标准。著有《空气源热泵——北方清洁供热的绿色革命》（中国建筑工业出版社，2026）。

### Zhichao Wang

Professor-level Senior Researcher and doctoral supervisor. Chief Engineer, Jianke Environment and Energy Technology Co., Ltd.; Deputy Director, Institute of Air Conditioning, China Academy of Building Research (CABR); member of SAC/TC143 (national technical committee for HVAC and air-cleaning equipment).

Long engaged in testing evaluation and standardization of HVAC and heat pump equipment, researching field performance, part-load seasonal efficiency, and data-driven prediction methods for air-source heat pumps. Co-drafted national standards including GB/T 14296 (*Air cooling and air heating coils*) and GB/T 14294 (*Central-station air-handling units*). Author of *Air Source Heat Pumps: The Green Revolution in Clean Heating for Northern China* (China Architecture & Building Press, 2026).
